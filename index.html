Good to know

Our salon policies

A few house rules that keep things running smoothly — and fair — for our stylists and every guest.

Chemical guarantee

Every chemical service is guaranteed — because we use Keune products exclusively, top to bottom.

Saturday appointments

Saturdays are reserved for existing customers and special events, and require a deposit to book.

24-hour cancellations

Plans change — just give your stylist at least 24 hours' notice. Missed appointments without notice are charged before a new one can be booked.

A respectful space

We're a family-friendly salon, and we hold that standard for our staff too: zero tolerance for negativity toward the team.
